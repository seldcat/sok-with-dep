Roles

Sysadmins and PC chairs have full control over all site operations. Sysadmins need not be members of the PC. There’s always at least one administrator (sysadmin or chair).

Collaborators and other affiliations

Please list potential conflicts of interest. We use this information when assigning reviews. This includes past advisors and students, people with the same affiliation, and any recent (~2 years) coauthors and collaborators.

Give one conflict per line, using parentheses for affiliations and institutions.
Examples: “Ping Yen Zhang (INRIA)”, “All (University College London)”

Tags
